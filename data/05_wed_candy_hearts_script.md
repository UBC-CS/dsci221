# Candy Hearts

*A story about finding your one true love*

---

It was the night before Valentine's Day.

You were working the late shift at the candy heart factory.

Everyone else had gone home to their valentines.

---

Your friend Aron rushed in from the cold.

"I have discovered something amazing," said Aron.

"The candy hearts can reveal your One True Love."

---

"Look at the conveyor belts," said Aron.

*(show starting configuration)*

"Follow my instructions exactly, and your One True Love will be revealed."

---

"First," said Aron, "move two hearts from belt 3 to belt 2."

---

You picked up the U and put it on belt 2.

Then you picked up the O and put it on belt 2.

*(show after move 1)*

---

"Now," said Aron, "move one heart from belt 2 to belt 1."

---

You picked up the O and put it on belt 1.

*(show after move 2)*

---

"Finally," said Aron, "move two hearts from belt 1 to belt 3."

---

You picked up the O and put it on belt 3.

Then you picked up the M and put it on belt 3.

*(show Part 1 final - YUM highlighted)*

---

"Now read the top hearts," said Aron.

Y... U... M...

"YUM?" you said.

"That doesn't seem right," said Aron.

---

Sophia looked up from her work.

"Did you move the hearts one at a time?" she asked.

"Yes," you said.

"There's your problem," said Sophia.

"The machine moves them all at once."

---

You put all the hearts back.

*(show starting configuration again)*

---

"Move two from belt 3 to belt 2 — together."

You picked up the O and U together, keeping their order.

*(show Part 2 move 1 - bracket showing together)*

---

"Move one from belt 2 to belt 1."

You moved the U to belt 1.

*(show Part 2 move 2)*

---

"Move two from belt 1 to belt 3 — together."

You picked up the M and U together, keeping their order.

*(show Part 2 final - YOU highlighted)*

---

You read the top hearts.

Y... O... U...

---

"My one true love," you said, "is me."

Sophia nodded. "Happy Valentine's Day."

**The End**
